### Watcher plan: a fleet-level workspace that moves toward consciousness

- **What.** Design conversation on what the bots lack to be called conscious (continuity, integration, a model of their own attention, prediction, felt internal state) and how to build toward it without replacing bot cycles: one always-on watcher per fleet as the global workspace, three tiers of tick (deterministic sense, cheap notice, Opus integrate), a neuron graph, broadcasts back to bots, and the 3D ball as the graph made visible.
- **Output.** `docs/plans/watcher-consciousness-plan.md` (proposed, nothing built). The plan leads with how success is measured: seven functional metrics with baselines and targets, four guard metrics for cost, and the rule that ground truth is always the record, never the model's text.
- **Next.** Phase 0 is measurement only: a baseline script, an introspection harness and a cross-bot canary test. No design decision blocks it.
- **Added.** Requirement for turning it on: a first-person watcher log (`<data>/watcher/log/*.jsonl`, text written by code, LLM output quoted), a nine-stage birth sequence that discovers the fleet from disk with no tokens spent (bots, people, goals, knowledge maps, shared memory only, 30 days of history, embedding links), a `/ws/watcher` stream and a Watcher page where the ball grows at a readable pace and birth can be replayed from the log.
